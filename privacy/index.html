Nyan Flasher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Nyan Flasher is a firmware-flashing utility for ESP32 hardware. This policy covers what the app does and does not do with your data.

USB access

The app requests USB host permission solely to communicate with ESP32 devices you connect over USB-C OTG, for the purpose of flashing firmware. No data from connected devices is collected, transmitted, or stored beyond what's needed to complete the flash operation on your device.

Network access

The app fetches firmware files and channel/catalogue metadata from URLs you add or select (including GitHub's public API and release assets, and any custom channel you configure). These requests go directly from your device to the relevant host (e.g. GitHub) and are not proxied through or logged by us.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers. Channel preferences and settings are stored locally on your device only.

Third parties

When you add a GitHub-based channel, requests are made directly to GitHub's API and are subject to GitHub's own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the contact details on the Nyan Devices storefront.
